# Fallow Valley — design capture

Post-apocalyptic homage to Stardew Valley: a war hero returns to find the family
farm abandoned after raiders killed his parents. Western arc — organize the town,
grow tongue-in-cheek mutant nuclear food. Fallout meets Stardew Valley.

The art in this folder is the style showcase (see `gen-hero.mjs` / `gen-farm.mjs`,
exports under `assets/claude-sprites/fallow-hero` and `fallow-farm`). When the game
gets its own repo, this doc moves with it.

## Pillars (owner-set, 2026-08-17)

1. **Merged inventory** — Stardew's grid meets Fallout's categories (Weapons /
   Aid / Seeds / Junk). Carry weight matters; seed packets ride a bandolier.
2. **Fallout turn-based combat** — action points, called shots. The hoe is a
   melee weapon with a sentimental damage bonus.
3. **Crop planting is core** — till, plant, irradiate, harvest. Mutant crops feed
   the town, fuel the economy, and occasionally fight back.

## Style contract (locked from prior-art study)

- **Fallout 1/2 mood**: desaturated olive/rust/dust, no outlines on distant
  forms, checkerboard-dithered ground shadows, ~5-head realistic proportions.
- **Stardew construction**: per-plank/per-shingle value wobble, selective
  outlines in dark-of-local-color (never black), 3–4 step hue-shifted ramps,
  trim accents.
- **Fusion rule**: saturation is a budget — only radiation green (#99e550) and
  dusk ember (#df7126) get to be loud. If it glows, it matters.
- Palette: db-32. Working set documented in the showcase artifact.
